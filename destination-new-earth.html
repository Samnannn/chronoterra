The journey

By the year 3000 the old argument between city and wilderness has been settled by dissolving the border. The towers are planted. The canopy is infrastructure. Light is grown, not switched on, and the streets above the clouds are quieter than the ground ever was.

This is the Board’s most speculative offering besides the Hadean one, and the most hopeful. You will dine in the lattice-forest at dusk, ride a lift to a ring-city that hangs where the sky thins, and spend an afternoon in a public conversation with a municipal intelligence that has opinions about your shoes.

It is a story about tomorrow. We have no idea if the real tomorrow will look like it, and we hope it looks better.

Fact & fiction

What we know. What we imagine.

Imagined. A story about tomorrow, not a prediction. We keep the two columns separate on purpose.

Context

  • This destination is entirely imagined. There are no historical events to report.
  • Real-world context: vertical gardens, urban rewilding and orbital engineering are active areas of present-day research and design.

The fiction

  • The city, its people and its machines are invented for this itinerary. Nothing here is a forecast.
  • The photographs show real structures today, used to suggest a future that does not yet exist.

Sample itinerary

The shape of your stay.

A representative plan. Final timings are fixed at your pre-departure briefing, and the Board reserves the right to be surprised.

  1. Arrival

    Ring Gate

    Step out into warm, humid air and a city that smells faintly of rain on leaves. Your clothes will adapt. Your expectations will take longer.

  2. Ascent

    The planted towers

    A guided walk up through terraced forest floors, each one a different climate.

  3. Dinner

    Beneath the lattice-trees

    A long table, a canopy that glows in time with the conversation below it.

  4. Orbit

    The ring district

    A lift ride to the thin edge of the sky. Mind the silence; it is intentional.

  5. Departure

    Return gate

    Hand back your adaptive garments. The city will remember you fondly, in the way cities do.

Before you go.

Fictional guidance for an imaginary Board.

ADV-05Fictional advisory

Never assume that the passage of time is synchronised between eras.

An afternoon in one century may be a fortnight in another. Consult your Drift Table before making dinner plans, and never promise anyone you will be back “in a minute.”

ADV-06Fictional advisory

Chronological jet lag is real.

Crossing centuries unsettles the body’s sense of when it is. Expect odd appetites, unwarranted nostalgia and a feeling that you have forgotten something. You have: it is called Tuesday. Drink water and keep a field note.

ADV-12Fictional advisory

Report any encounter with an alternate version of yourself.

Remain calm, do not shake hands, and do not compare notes. Alternate yourself will be equally suspicious. File Form 9-A within the hour and request a quiet room.
