Travel advisories

Every era has its hazards.

Time travel is an extraordinary privilege. Arriving unprepared is an avoidable tragedy.

This is fiction. These advisories are world-building for an imaginary tourism board. They are not real safety guidance, and no time travel is offered.

The classification system

Five classes of temporal risk.

Every destination and every advisory carries a class. Higher classes demand more preparation, more paperwork and more humility.

  1. ITemporal riskClass I · SereneSerene

    A postcard with no consequences. Sensible shoes advised.

    Carbon-Free City

  2. IITemporal riskClass II · MildMild

    Minor etiquette, minor jet lag. Resolvable with apologies.

    Mughal Court, Last Day of Wi-Fi

  3. IIITemporal riskClass III · ElevatedElevated

    Environmental or cultural hazards. Briefing attendance mandatory.

    New Earth, Alexandria

  4. IVTemporal riskClass IV · SevereSevere

    Rapid change, serious hazards. Licensed guide required.

    No current destination

  5. VTemporal riskClass V · ExtremeExtreme

    Board escort mandatory. Not survivable unassisted.

§ 1

Before Departure

The most dangerous part of any journey is the part where you are certain you have prepared.

!
Checklist requiredComplete every item below before your gate time. Gates do not wait; they merely re-open.

Pre-departure checklist

0 of 8

  1. 01

    ADV-01 Fictional advisory

    Verify your return coordinate before departure.

    Read it aloud. Read it again. Have your guide read it. A return coordinate that is correct to six decimal places is a promise; one that is correct to five is a story you will tell at dinner parties, if you get home.

    Temporal riskClass II · MildApplies to: Mughal Court

  2. 02

    ADV-02 Fictional advisory

    Declare your timeline of origin.

    Your Board manifest must record the timeline you are departing from. Travellers who cannot name their timeline will be asked, politely, to wait in a very calm room.

    Temporal riskClass I · SereneApplies to: Last Day of Wi-Fi

  3. 03

    ADV-03 Fictional advisory

    Pack for the era, not for the weather app.

    Natural fibres only. No logos, no zips, no visible stitching from any decade you are not visiting. See the Packing List for era-specific guidance.

    Temporal riskClass II · MildApplies to: Alexandria

  4. 04

    ADV-04 Fictional advisory

    Attend the Orientation Seminar in full.

    It lasts ninety minutes and includes a short film. The film is excellent and deeply upsetting. Sit near the front.

    Temporal riskClass I · SereneApplies to: Carbon-Free City

§ 2

During Your Visit

The era will not adapt to you. You are, as the Board politely reminds you, the anachronism.

!
Caution · you are the anachronismThe era will not adapt to you. Look, listen, and leave no trace of the twenty-first century.
  1. 05

    ADV-05 Fictional advisory

    Never assume that the passage of time is synchronised between eras.

    An afternoon in one century may be a fortnight in another. Consult your Drift Table before making dinner plans, and never promise anyone you will be back “in a minute.”

    Temporal riskClass III · ElevatedApplies to: New Earth, Carbon-Free City, The First Dawn

  2. 06

    ADV-06 Fictional advisory

    Chronological jet lag is real.

    Crossing centuries unsettles the body’s sense of when it is. Expect odd appetites, unwarranted nostalgia and a feeling that you have forgotten something. You have: it is called Tuesday. Drink water and keep a field note.

    Temporal riskClass II · MildApplies to: New Earth, Last Day of Wi-Fi

  3. 07

    ADV-07 Fictional advisory

    Do not remove artifacts from protected historical sites.

    Not a coin. Not a pebble. Not a fragment of mosaic. Anything you carry out of an era will, at best, be confiscated at the gate and, at worst, be discovered in a museum with your fingerprints on it.

    Temporal riskClass III · ElevatedApplies to: Mughal Court, Alexandria

  4. 08

    ADV-08 Fictional advisory

    Speak less than you know.

    Idioms, slang and accurate opinions about the future are the quickest ways to be remembered. You are visiting to be forgotten.

    Temporal riskClass I · SereneApplies to: Carbon-Free City

§ 3

Historical Integrity

We borrow the past. We do not edit it.

!
Stop · Class IV advisoriesLicensed guide required. These rules protect more than your return. They protect everyone’s yesterday.
  1. 09

    ADV-09 Fictional advisory

    Do not alter events of historical significance.

    The Board’s Integrity Desk monitors every itinerary. If you find yourself wondering whether a small intervention might be harmless, it is not harmless, and you are already on the list.

    Temporal riskClass IV · SevereApplies to: Alexandria, The First Dawn

  2. 10

    ADV-10 Fictional advisory

    Never disclose future events to historical figures.

    Not a hint. Not a joke. Not a polite “you’ll love what happens next.” People remember who told them things, and so do their biographers.

    Temporal riskClass IV · SevereApplies to: Mughal Court

  3. 11

    ADV-11 Fictional advisory

    Do not introduce technology.

    No devices, no clever tools, no “just a quick demonstration.” If you must record, use era-safe materials: ink, charcoal, memory, and a very good sense of when to stop talking.

    Temporal riskClass III · ElevatedApplies to: Alexandria

§ 4

Temporal Anomalies

When something is strange, assume it is. When something is familiar, assume it more.

!
Anomaly watchReport. Do not repair. Strange things, if left alone, are usually only strange.
  1. 12

    ADV-12 Fictional advisory

    Report any encounter with an alternate version of yourself.

    Remain calm, do not shake hands, and do not compare notes. Alternate yourself will be equally suspicious. File Form 9-A within the hour and request a quiet room.

    Temporal riskClass III · ElevatedApplies to: New Earth, Last Day of Wi-Fi, The First Dawn

  2. 13

    ADV-13 Fictional advisory

    Never accept an unverified invitation from your future self.

    They sound very convincing. They know your middle name, your favourite sandwich and exactly what you want to hear. Verify via the Board’s authentication line and, if in doubt, decline politely and leave the room.

    Temporal riskClass IV · SevereApplies to: New Earth

  3. 14

    ADV-14 Fictional advisory

    If a clock runs backward, do not wind it.

    Observe, note the time (such as it is) and move away. It is not your clock, and nobody will thank you for fixing it.

    Temporal riskClass II · Mild

§ 5

Return Protocol

Arrival is optional. Departure is the whole point.

!
Return protocol in forceDepart by the gate you arrived at. Declare what you carry. Drink the tea.
  1. 15

    ADV-15 Fictional advisory

    Return only through the gate you arrived by.

    Other gates may look identical. They are not. Some of them lead to the same place a few minutes earlier, which is worse than it sounds.

    Temporal riskClass II · MildApplies to: Mughal Court, The First Dawn

  2. 16

    ADV-16 Fictional advisory

    Submit to the Chronological Decompression Quarter-Hour.

    A mandatory fifteen minutes in a pleasant room with tea. You will want to leave. Do not leave.

    Temporal riskClass I · SereneApplies to: Last Day of Wi-Fi

  3. 17

    ADV-17 Fictional advisory

    File your field note within 72 hours.

    The Board’s archive depends on honest, specific, slightly embarrassing accounts. The best entries are published in the Field Journal.

    Temporal riskClass I · SereneApplies to: Carbon-Free City

Emergency return protocol

If something goes wrong: R.E.T.U.R.N.

Memorise it. Print it. Sew it into something. Six steps, in order.

  1. R01

    Remain calm

    Stop. Breathe. Nobody has ever fixed a timeline while sprinting.

  2. E02

    Establish your coordinate

    Read the return coordinate sewn into your hem. Say it aloud.

  3. T03

    Trigger the Tether

    Hold the beacon for five seconds until it chimes. It will not chime on its first attempt.

  4. U04

    Unclasp local anchors

    Let go of artifacts, promises and any strong opinions about the local cuisine.

  5. R05

    Reach the gate

    Walk, don’t run, to your arrival gate. If it has moved, wait. It will return.

  6. N06

    Notify the Board

    On arrival, report to the Decompression Room, and tell the truth.

Advisory FAQ

Questions you will definitely ask.

More in the Paradox FAQ.

Are these advisories real safety guidance?

No. They are fictional world-building for a design project. Time travel is not available, and for real travel advice you should consult your national travel authority.

Who writes the advisories?

The Board’s Department of Cautionary Tales: three clerks, an archivist, and a kettle of considerable age. All of them have been very careful and some of them have been very wrong.

What does a Class IV rating actually mean?

It means the destination demands a licensed guide, a briefing in full and the understanding that the Board’s rescue window is narrow. Class V adds a mandatory escort and a signature on a document with an unusual number of pages.

What if I accidentally change something small?

The past is elastic. A dropped coin or an unwise remark is typically absorbed. File Form 7-b at the gate, describe it honestly and expect to be asked three follow-up questions. If the Integrity Desk calls twice, please sit down.

Is the Board liable for paradoxes?

The Board is liable for your return. Paradoxes, as the small print on every manifest explains, remain the traveller’s own.
