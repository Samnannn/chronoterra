The journey

In Alternate Timeline B-27, a quiet engineering shrug will end the world’s wireless networks at midnight. Nobody knows why. Everybody knows when. This is the last full day of signal, and you have been granted a seat.

Morning is panicked backups and strangers asking for directions out loud. By noon, somebody has organised a farewell group call that nobody can leave. At dusk, in the half-lit streets, the pavement fills with people discovering what a conversation is for.

This is a satire, not a prophecy. The premise is invented, and the Board takes no position on whether you should put your phone down at home.

Highlights

  1. Noon: the farewell group call that never ends
  2. A paper-map navigation lesson from an enthusiastic stranger
  3. Dusk: the street fills for the Great Unplugging
  4. Midnight: a minute of real, shared silence

Fact & fiction

What we know. What we imagine.

A timeline that never happened. Pure invention. We keep the two columns separate on purpose.

Context

  • This destination is fiction. No known event threatens the world’s Wi-Fi networks.
  • Real-world context: the premise satirises how much of daily life now depends on connectivity.

The fiction

  • Everything about Timeline B-27 is invented, including the shrug.
  • Sample photographs show quiet streets at night in the present day. They stand in for a world holding its breath.

Sample itinerary

The shape of your stay.

A representative plan. Final timings are fixed at your pre-departure briefing, and the Board reserves the right to be surprised.

  1. 08:00

    Arrival

    You arrive among people checking their phones with a new, desperate tenderness.

  2. 10:30

    The last map

    A stranger teaches you to read a paper street map. They are thrilled. You are terrified.

  3. 12:00

    The group call

    The farewell call begins. It will last for hours. No one remembers how to hang up.

  4. 19:00

    The Great Unplugging

    The street fills. People talk. A man teaches a dog to fetch with an old router.

  5. 23:59

    The Silence

    A minute of shared quiet. Then, somewhere, a radio. Then, gently, everyone laughs.

ADV-06Fictional advisory

Chronological jet lag is real.

Crossing centuries unsettles the body’s sense of when it is. Expect odd appetites, unwarranted nostalgia and a feeling that you have forgotten something. You have: it is called Tuesday. Drink water and keep a field note.
